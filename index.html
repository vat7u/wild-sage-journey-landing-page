01 / The Origin

From Dimmed to Whole.

Beth Eberle, B.S., CHWC
Founder, Wild Sage Journey

Beth Eberle — In Practice & Presence

There is a particular kind of silence that follows the unraveling of a life you thought was permanent.

When my marriage ended, it wasn't simply the dissolution of a partnership—it was the quiet realization that somewhere along the road, I had traded away my own intuition. I had allowed myself to feel diminished, muted, and defined by stories about who I was supposed to be. For years, I existed in surviving mode, doing what was expected while my internal spark grew faint.

Healing did not arrive in an instant breakthrough. It arrived through the patient, somatic reclamation of my daily life: nourishing food, mindful movement, nervous-system restoration, and confronting the beliefs that kept me small. I made the conscious choice to resign from my retail management career and return to university to earn a degree in Alternative Medicine.

Following my formal degree, I spent five rigorous months immersing myself in traditional and somatic wellness disciplines across the Netherlands, Morocco, and Argentina. That journey solidified what I believe today: true wellness is not about fixing broken pieces—it is about relighting the inner flame that circumstance tried to extinguish.

Through Wild Sage Journey, I do not hand you a generic formula. I walk beside you as an anchor while you remember how to trust yourself again.
